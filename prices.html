Understanding Market Data

Learn how to read and interpret cryptocurrency market indicators.

Market Cap

Market capitalization is the total value of all coins in circulation. It is calculated by multiplying the current price by the circulating supply. A higher market cap generally indicates a more established and stable cryptocurrency.

24h Change

The percentage change in price over the last 24 hours. Positive values indicate the price has increased, while negative values show a decrease. This helps traders and investors gauge short-term momentum.

Trading Volume

Volume represents the total dollar value of all trades in the last 24 hours. High volume indicates strong interest and liquidity. Low volume can mean the price is more easily manipulated by large trades.
